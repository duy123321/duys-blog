Your friend is heading to New York. That one coworker you barely talk to is heading to New York. That asshole who thinks he's hot shit is going to New York. That quiet dude from college is heading to New York. Your love is heading to New York. Hell, she might already be in New York. Why the f*ck is everyone going to New York???

I think I'd be out of mind to suggest New York isn't or wasn't a hot destination for young adults. I mean its mythical at this point. Globally recognizable, culturally ubiquitous, and veneered amongst generations of people. New York is New York. I don't blame anyone for imagining a life there. To live in a big city is still a treat in its own.

Yet, to many of those living outside of this great city. Why does EVERYONE want to move there?

Historically, there are at least two different draws to New York after its crowning as a major city of the world, not just the US. The first being work which is fairly self explanatory as a city. The second being culture. Doesn't really matter what kind of discipline you practice, if you were an artist, you had a home in New York City. Musician? Painter? Something else entirely? New York is a great place to start that journey. For thr 80's, 90's, 00's, people flocked to the city to immerse themselves in the culture but often to take part in it as well. To be amongst other aspiring New York creators.

By no means has that mindset ended. Artist still head to New York because art thrives in New York. Yet, there seems to be a even larger wave of individuals migrating to New York than ever before. Those that aren't coming to make something out of themselves. Those that don't need the opportunity that only New York can provide. They aren't there because they have to. They aren't there to build themselves up. But rather they go to New York just to be in New York.

There is an ever present feeling of FOMO (Fear of Missing Out) when someone speaks about making the move to New York. Now FOMO isn't necessarily a bad thing. It is by nature a human reaction, in which all humans reactions are rooted in some sense of survival or at least as a guide for our decision making. Ignorance to it however, leads us to make decision that puts the interest of others beyond our own. It's the transplant of outside wants and desires into our own psyche. It's existence must be reflected. 

New York and the emotions to being in big city is heavily intertwined with FOMO. Everyone is there. The mythical late night adventures, spiritual journeys, and character growth is an indirectly advertised perk of situating yourself in the concrete jungle. I would be foolish to suggest New York won't give you such an experience. It would in fact be disingenuous of me to say so. But I want to make a small argument, that the fulfilling life you seek, may be else where. Maybe I'm a pathological contrarian and I am rallying against it because everyone else wants to go. Regardless, I shall make my attempt, not to convince you otherwise, but to poke at your desires and encourage reflection on where you may end up.

Without a doubt, FOMO is more impactful post COVID. I do plan on writing about this in length but I want to focus on a small instance of it being, the FOMO of New York. But why? The city is "aesthetic". When I use this term I mean it in the modern way. You take a picture of the city. It's presence is undeniable. The feeling of New York permeates. How do I best describe this phenomena? I'm not sure but I can describe how you ought to feel. You can imagine yourself there. You can imagine your ideal life there. How you would walk through the streets, live in that apartment, and visit those restaurants and cafes. 

The fear of not being in New York. I speak of it as if its some virus. As more folks start to feel this pull and desire, those around them also get infected. It's a fully harmless virus though. For most folks, you are at risk of a fun and memorable years of young adult existence. But I want to propose the consideration of another option or at least the reflection of purpose and reason.
