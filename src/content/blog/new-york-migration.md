Your friend is heading to New York. That one coworker you barely talk to is heading to New York. That asshole who thinks he's hot shit is going to New York. That quiet dude from college is heading to New York. Your love is heading to New York. Hell, she might already be in New York. Why the f*ck is everyone going to New York???

I think I'd be out of mind to suggest New York isn't or wasn't a hot destination for young adults. I mean its mythical at this point. Globally recognizable, culturally ubiquitous, and veneered amongst generations of people. New York is New York. I don't blame anyone for imagining a life there. To live in a big city is still a treat in its own.

Yet, to many of those living outside of this great city. Why does EVERYONE want to move there?

Historically, there were two different draws to New York after its crowning as a major city of the world, not just the US. The first being work which is fairly self explanatory as a city. The second being culture. Doesn't really matter what kind of discipline you practice, if you were an artist, you had a home in New York City. Musician? Painter? Something else entirely? New York is a great place to start that journey. For thr 80's, 90's, 00's, people flocked to the city to immerse themselves in the culture but often to take part in it as well. To be amongst other aspiring New York creators.

By no means has that mindset ended. Artist still head to New York because art thrives in New York. Yet, there seems to be a even larger wave of individuals migrating to New York than ever before. Those that aren't coming to make something out of themselves. Those that don't need the opportunity that only New York can provide. They aren't there because they have to. They aren't there to build themselves up. But rather they go to New York just to be in New York.

The fear of not being in New York. Speak of it as if its some virus. As more folks start to feel this pull and desire, those around them also get infected.
